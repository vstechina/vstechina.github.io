VSTE manufacture Co.,ltd established in 2009, is the owner of the VSTE brand, specializing in high-quality engineering hardware and home decoration hardware. The company operates a 40,000-square-meter modern manufacturing facility, dedicated to providing premium products and professional ODM and OEM services to high-end clients worldwide. Our products are certified by internationally recognized standards, including UL, FDA, and CE, ensuring that every product meets the highest global quality requirements.

The VSTE brand is registered in Italy, and we serve a wide range of high-end clients in North America (Canada, the US), Europe (Germany, France, the UK, Sweden, Italy), and the Middle East (Dubai, Saudi Arabia). Among our prestigious partners, we are a trusted supplier to Greif, a high-end lock brand in Germany.

Precision Production Equipment

We operate 5 advanced CNC machines, ensuring high accuracy and efficiency in manufacturing.

Advanced Production Lines

Our factory is equipped with 6 advanced production lines, including a specialized German production line that meets the highest standards for premium products.

Skilled Workforce

With 200 experienced workers, we ensure stability in production and consistency in product quality.

Product Laboratory

Our laboratory is equipped with salt spray testing equipment to rigorously test corrosion resistance, ensuring that our products perform excellently in various environments.

Technical R&D Team

Our professional R&D team is dedicated to continuous product innovation and technological breakthroughs, providing customized solutions for our clients.
